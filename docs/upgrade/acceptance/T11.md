# T11 acceptance: biomass, medium and physiology contracts

**Status: ACCEPTED**

Biomass and medium assumptions now have explicit source, applicability, units,
known or unresolved GAM/NGAM, trace components, nutrient limits, oxygen state,
temperature and control role. Canonical JSON content produces stable contract
hashes; rich/minimal and aerobic/anaerobic declarations cannot share a hash.

Medium application is direction aware. Reverse-coded exchanges receive the
correct uptake bound, and multiple ports for the same chemical share one budget
by selecting one canonical uptake port and closing uptake through its aliases.
Secretion remains available under `no_uptake`; full boundary closure is a separate
operation. Exchange, demand, sink and audit-only drains retain distinct roles.

Every negative biomass coefficient remains a required precursor, including trace
coefficients below common absolute solver tolerances. Non-growth diagnostics use
annotation gap, transport gap, chemistry conflict, template mismatch or numerical
inconclusive classifications without deleting a precursor or switching biomass.

Reference-derived anaerobic tasks are labeled as prior inputs rather than external
phenotype evidence. Reference growth ceilings require the `reference_assisted`
track and mark any changed model as calibration-derived; they do not claim
phenotype accuracy or energy-cycle removal.
