# T12 acceptance: scoped QC and independent certificate

**Status: ACCEPTED**

Quality checks now identify their profile, scope and required status. The registry
covers SBML structure, chemical fields, stoichiometry, strict closed material,
energy carriers, no-uptake with excretion, joint biomass reachability, medium
phenotype, GPR evidence, provenance and export consistency. Missing carrier or
compartment coverage is `not_covered` and blocks a required certificate.

Numerical outcomes preserve absolute residual, relative residual and critical
trace-flux resolution. Missing or unreliable information produces
`numerical_inconclusive`; it cannot become a pass by tightening a single absolute
tolerance.

The independent verifier reloads exported SBML and binds results to model semantic
hash, file SHA-256, scope hash, solver and numerical policy. Any required failure,
timeout, solver failure, numerical failure, inconclusive result or uncovered scope
blocks the certificate. Bounds, GPR and medium mutations are detected after export.
External validation remains false unless a dedicated phenotype scope passes with
independent evidence.

- Focused certificate, CER, semantic and contract suite: **55 passed**.
- Known synthetic violations produced zero required-profile false passes.
- Ruff over `src`, `tests` and `scripts`: passed.
- `git diff --check`: passed.
